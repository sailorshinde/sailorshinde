## 🎯 SUCCESS FACTORS

### What Makes Implementation Successful?

✅ **Clear Ownership:** Assign specific people to each task  
✅ **Weekly Tracking:** Review progress every Friday  
✅ **Quick Communication:** Fix blockers same week  
✅ **Team Alignment:** Everyone knows their role  
✅ **Realistic Timeline:** Don't compress too aggressively  
✅ **Testing Discipline:** Validate changes before live  
✅ **Measurement Focus:** Track metrics religiously  
✅ **Stakeholder Updates:** Keep leadership informed  

### Common Pitfalls to Avoid

❌ Assigning tasks without clear ownership  
❌ Skipping testing to move faster  
❌ Implementing everything at once (causes issues)  
❌ Not monitoring results  
❌ Losing momentum between phases  
❌ Changing direction mid-implementation  
❌ Not communicating with team  
❌ Setting unrealistic timeline expectations
